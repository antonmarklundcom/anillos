## How Anillos can compete

1. Restore real buying paths first. Production connects to MySQL, but actual catalog queries fail. Content and the hero cannot substitute for real inventory, contact and safe checkout.
2. Test a focused affordable steel/silver segment against Paraguay Keyword Planner data. Competitor prices provide a comparison set, not our retail price. Calculate supplier, packing, payment, delivery exposure and margin before pricing.
3. Make every SKU comparable: one ring versus two; composition/coating; stone identity; diameter, width and profile; independent sizes; exact total; original photos; stock/enquiry status; adjustment and delivery terms.
4. Photograph the real ring in macro, from the side and worn for scale. The generated hero remains editorial and cannot verify a stocked product's material or appearance. Keep the whole ring visible and navigation immediate on phones.
5. Build five useful clusters: steel, silver 925, silver pairs, gold pairs and commitment. Link their guides and real products. Consolidate synonyms into these destinations rather than generating 80 thin landing pages.
6. Match useful sizing help. Current code adds diameter/circumference geometry, guide contents and talla/argolla search handling. Commercial-size conversion needs the actual supplier's scale. G&A already has sophisticated tools: this is a baseline to match, not an exclusive capability.
7. Put verified contact, total pair cost, actual delivery quote and exchange/adjustment terms beside the purchase decision. Add real reviews after purchases and a business profile only with valid identity/location.
8. Choose offers by contribution margin. Start with a small number of supplier-backed bundles. Bank financing is a later agreement. Use keyword data and existing admin/order records initially; no GTM or analytics was introduced.

## First owner-supplied Keyword Planner sample

The pasted sample reports 3,600 for `añillo de compromiso`, 1,900 for `añillo de promesa`, and 480 for `anillos de boda`. Use the correctly spelled `anillo` in website copy. Location, reporting period and variant grouping are not yet confirmed, so these are provisional supplied figures, not verified Paraguay market totals. Swedish UI/currency does not establish the targeting location. Do not add spelling/close-variant rows together as independent demand.

`Anillo de compromiso` means an engagement ring (Swedish förlovningsring); `anillo de promesa` is a promise ring (löftesring), and `anillos de boda`/`alianzas de matrimonio` are wedding rings (vigselringar). [GIA's promise-ring explanation](https://4cs.gia.edu/en-us/blog/guide-to-promise-ring-meaning/) distinguishes relationship commitment from formal engagement; marriage is not always the intent of a promise ring.

If Paraguay targeting is confirmed, prioritize engagement and promise intent within the broader keyword review, while retaining wedding pairs as a commercially specific category. Give promise rings their own destination when real suitable products exist, rather than relabeling them as engagement rings or creating an empty collection. Do not target dress/centerpiece terms on a ring store. Google Ads competition labels do not establish organic SEO difficulty.
