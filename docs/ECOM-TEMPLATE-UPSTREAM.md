## Current supplier-ready additions

This is a **store implementation**, not an upstream merge. Candidate mechanisms must be reviewed with the template's existing contract before proposing a neutral version:

- `src/lib/product-attributes.ts` and versioned nullable schema fields: validation and storage are reusable; ring-specific diameter/pair fields should be optional and documented. Only explicitly verified identifiers may reach structured data.
- `src/lib/public-product-facts.ts`: useful boundary between safe public specifications and private supplier evidence. Supplier references, source URLs, internal prices and contact details must stay in admin/import.
- `src/store/product-seo-readiness.ts` and `src/components/admin/product-seo-checklist.tsx`: advisory completeness checks, separate stock/enquiry/showcase/concept handling and genuine-photo distinctions. They do not authorize publication or change reservations/orders. Ring sizing and gemstone checks need a configurable strategy before generic adoption.
- `src/store/product-guidance.ts`: safe public FAQ/recommendation generation from explicitly verified attributes. Its current answers and guide routes are jewellery-specific; do not ship them as a default for all stores.
- `src/components/admin/seo-coverage-dashboard.tsx`: generic filtering/table behavior can be reused with neutral source metadata. `src/content/keyword-coverage.ts` remains this store's private editorial ledger; all 166 labels/volumes are sourced from `docs/SEO-CONTENT-IMPLEMENTATION.md` and do not belong in the base template.
- Per-variant URLs, comparison and gallery improvements are candidates only when they preserve existing URL, keyboard, price visibility and product/test contracts. Comparison copy and jewellery presentation remain store skin. Root/technical-agent checks establish their implementation status.
- Payment-policy mechanisms may be configurable template features. This store's ueno-only transfer choice, verified recipient details and delivery/collection decisions remain store policy. Pagopar remains optional and requires a configured, tested integration; never enable it by copied credentials.
- Owner welcome/setup handoff and admin workspace navigation can be proposed separately with role isolation, per-account menu persistence, print behavior and synthetic browser evidence preserved. The keyword dashboard uses the owner-only `usuarios` capability; a template extraction should explicitly choose its permission contract.

## External setup and email boundary

The launch checklist remains separate from an upstream patch: verified supplier inventory and image rights, public WhatsApp/email, Cloudinary for product photos and transfer receipts, actual bank recipient/payment policy, shipping coverage/prices, approved policies, backups and authenticated jobs. A deployment also needs its own private database/session/setup/cron configuration. Optional automated messages and Pagopar need their own verified setup; they are not prerequisites for every store.

**Ordinary email recovery is not enabled by this document or dashboard.** It remains future implementation and setup until a sender, domain authorization and actual delivery have been verified. Current Cloudflare documentation and a Claude prompt are recorded separately in [the email handoff](CLOUDFLARE-EMAIL-HANDOFF.md); no DNS, email provider credentials or messages are changed here. The current manual owner recovery method is the existing authenticated-owner or guarded `create-owner`/setup process against the correct database. Do not present a planned sender or password-reset UI as operational recovery.
