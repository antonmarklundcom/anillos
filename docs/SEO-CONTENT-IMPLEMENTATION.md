## Remaining gaps and exclusions

- A real assortment still requires supplier-confirmed SKU/model, metal/ley/finish, gem identity and origin/treatments, unit/pair price, actual size variants, stock, delivery and image rights. Design concepts are not merchant offers or evidence of these facts. Root is separately expanding local unpurchasable concepts from five to 24; this content task does not mutate the database or confirm any saleable product.
- Local-intent pages are limited to existing supported destinations. A city appearing in KWP does not prove a branch, pickup point, shipping quote or local service. Root owns any location/service architecture; this change does not manufacture Asunción/Luque branches.
- Ring-holder accessories, toe/children's rings and wholesale require an actual assortment/operating decision before creating commercial pages. Informational custom/measurement advice does not claim those products are sold.
- Watches, earrings, necklaces, bracelets, general wedding decorations/dresses/invitations, civil-marriage legal requirements and ceremonies are outside the current ring store. Mixed clusters may still contribute an eligible ring subtopic, as shown in the destination table.
- Competitor, designer, celebrity, fictional/fandom and branded model queries are not targets. `anillo antrax` is held for manual intent review, not published as a product identity.
- Several minor stone names are covered by concrete identity/selection advice within one section, not researched as individual gem encyclopaedia articles. A dedicated guide requires additional factual sourcing and a distinct customer need; a category requires a coherent verified assortment.
- No claim of covering all 166 clusters, capturing 220,440 searches, or guaranteeing Paraguayan rankings is made. Coverage supports discovery; indexing, competition, links, merchandising and useful verified inventory determine outcomes.
