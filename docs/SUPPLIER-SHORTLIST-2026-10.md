## Margin decisions

Asunción Joyas' public discount gives a useful ceiling, not a guaranteed quote: applying 30% to a Gs. 110,000 retail piece yields Gs. 77,000 acquisition and Gs. 33,000 gross spread (30%) before packaging, delivery, fees, defects, taxes and marketing. For the promotional band, hypothetical Gs. 84,000 acquisition against a Gs. 90,000 competitive selling price leaves only Gs. 6,000 (6.7%) before those costs. Confirm the discount base; do not assume promotional discounts combine.

Use actual quotes in this calculation:

`landed cost per saleable piece = (purchase + freight + clearance + nonrecoverable taxes + FX fees + QC losses) / saleable pieces`

`contribution = selling revenue on a consistent tax basis − landed cost − packaging − delivery subsidy − payment fees − returns allowance − customer acquisition cost`

For illustration only, a Gs. 150,000 selling price, 50% target contribution and Gs. 15,000 other variable costs allow at most Gs. 60,000 landed acquisition. This is not a proposed product price or an import-tax estimate. No FX or customs rate is invented.

Start with local samples and a few confirmed sizes; use a custom Luque quotation for pairs. Compare Brazilian quotes on total delivered cost. Consider Chinese steel bulk only after samples pass inspection and repeat demand justifies size inventory. Avoid dropshipping promises to Paraguay until the supplier confirms delivery and returns in writing.

## What the website can show now

`supplier-candidates-draft.csv` provides three concrete, sourced starting references. Its prefixed SKUs are internal draft keys derived from supplier references, not GTINs/MPNs. Price is empty, stock zero, quotation mode with hidden price, no photos and no verified facts. Import creates unpublished drafts; do not publish automatically or treat the source's retail price as your offer. No general wholesale lead without an exact model has been invented as a product row.

The local test preview has ten category routes and 24 clearly labelled design concepts. Concepts have no price, no inventory, no offers and no purchase button, and are excluded from indexing, sitemap and merchant feeds. Real merchandise is imported as a draft for review. Supplier URLs, tentative facts and public source prices do not become verified product facts or stock.

Before a draft goes live: choose the actual model and unit, obtain resale and photo permission, inspect a sample, confirm material/stone/measurements/sizes, set your own supported PYG price and stock (or quotation mode), and agree delivery/returns. AI imagery may illustrate an idea with a caption; it cannot substitute for genuine model evidence or become a merchant photo merely by changing a checkbox.
