## Additional usability and operational gaps

These are separate from the numbered defects and do not all require a bug fix.

1. **Search stops at 40 products.** There is no continuation or explanation that the displayed results are capped. This becomes relevant when the real assortment grows; queries.ts currently limits the result set.
2. **Indicative prices need consistent context.** An enquiry product's optional card price lacks the qualification used on the detail/comparison pages. This is conditional on Anton confirming and enabling a real price.
3. **Recently viewed prices are browser snapshots.** They are not refreshed or identified as historical. Future visible prices can differ from the current product; see recently-viewed.tsx around line 171.
4. **Facet counts exclude brand/price constraints.** Counts can exceed the current grid when those filters are active. The attribute facets otherwise support filtering; decide whether counts describe the whole category or the current selection.
5. **Comparison omits stone shape.** The catalogue now stores verified shape, but the public table does not compare it. Add it with the same unknown-value handling as other specifications.
6. **There are two different product enquiry routes.** The short WhatsApp link carries the chosen variant/SKU; the detailed composer carries the product and manually entered diameter, without the chosen variant. Unifying them would reduce clarification messages.
7. **Quote lines do not capture the chosen ring size.** The customer sheet lists model, quantity, unit and price. Exact size system and measurement need structured fields before quotations become useful fulfilment records. For a pair, represent two independent sizes while keeping the two-ring builder deferred.
8. **Linked enquiry exports have a narrower scope than the full workspace.** Supplier sample/performance records are not included in the per-enquiry export. The full private workspace export remains available. Exported files and external backups also retain copies until Anton manages them separately.
9. **Homepage speed has room to improve.** The latest saved desktop-home result was 2,720 ms LCP against a 2,500 ms local budget; previous local runs varied. No fresh performance benchmark was run in this audit. See [local audit notes](LOCAL-STOREFRONT-AUDIT.md).
10. **Windows audit cleanup remains a tooling limitation.** The prior audit wrote its report but could not confirm browser shutdown and exited 1. Its page findings must be read separately from runner completion; do not describe that run as an unconditional pass.
11. **Business identity and fulfilment remain owner facts.** Unset contact email/address/hours are omitted in the sampled local contact view rather than shown as invented details. Actual identity, channels, delivery, returns, resizing and engraving conditions still need Anton's decisions.
12. **The real assortment is not established.** The fixture has 24 protected concepts and twelve unpublished supplier candidates. Research references are not supplier agreements, confirmed stock, material verification or photo rights. Use the [supplier briefs](SUPPLIER-PRODUCT-BRIEFS.md) and [catalogue batch](SUPPLIER-CATALOGUE-BATCH-2026-10.md) for those outstanding facts.
13. **PR 18 requires migration 0025 before release.** The migration adds owner-workspace tables and catalogue image columns; the new catalogue queries need those columns. It was rehearsed locally only. This audit introduces no new schema work and does not authorise applying migrations.
14. **Live security, cron and backups are unverified here.** Anton must privately finish credential rotation/owner setup, schedule the documented jobs and confirm recovery. Follow the [owner guide's technical operations](OWNER-OPERATING-GUIDE.md#technical-operations-for-anton); the audit did not inspect or change those systems.

## Twenty coding ideas to increase sales

These extend what is already built. They require no mailbox, paid image generation or Meta/Google product setup. They keep enquiries non-binding and use confirmed facts wherever a price, material, service or delivery condition appears. Features can improve conversion and efficiency; they do not guarantee revenue without a real supply/fulfilment process.

| #   | Coding idea                                                                                                                   | Commercial purpose                                                                    |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **One product enquiry flow** carrying the selected SKU, size system and desired quantity into the detailed WhatsApp composer. | Reduce lost preferences and repeated questions.                                       |
| 2   | **A mobile sticky enquiry button** that opens the product composer and preserves its draft.                                   | Keep the next action accessible while a buyer reads a long product page.              |
| 3   | **Enquire about all compared models** directly from the comparison table.                                                     | Turn comparison into one clear conversation.                                          |
| 4   | **Show comparison differences first**, including verified shape and exact dimensions.                                         | Help customers decide between close alternatives.                                     |
| 5   | **Related models based on confirmed attributes**, with useful alternatives when no exact match exists.                        | Keep a buyer exploring relevant options.                                              |
| 6   | **Remember category position and filters** when returning from a product.                                                     | Make browsing several rings less tiring.                                              |
| 7   | **Search continuation and richer query parsing** for verified shape, width and size, with a visible result total.             | Let buyers find a growing real assortment.                                            |
| 8   | **Several named shortlists in the browser**, such as Para regalo or Para comparar.                                            | Help returning buyers keep separate shopping decisions organised.                     |
| 9   | **Share only selected favourites** in a clean, compact shortlist.                                                             | Make it easier to ask a partner or friend for an opinion.                             |
| 10  | **A guided measurement check** that compares two repeated measurements and explains uncertainty.                              | Reduce size mistakes before a quote; extend the existing calculator.                  |
| 11  | **Carry a guide measurement into an enquiry** with its method and an explicit unconfirmed label.                              | Avoid retyping and preserve useful size context.                                      |
| 12  | **Optional service choices tied to the actual product**, using owner-confirmed packaging, adjustment or engraving conditions. | Make relevant extras easy to discuss without invented offers.                         |
| 13  | **An engraving text helper** using supplier-confirmed limits, character rules and a simple text preview.                      | Catch unsuitable text early and simplify personalisation enquiries.                   |
| 14  | **Private, expiring customer quote links** showing a saved, allowlisted revision and a WhatsApp response action.              | Make a proposal easier to read on a phone; no checkout or payment acceptance.         |
| 15  | **Quote renewal workflow** flagging expiry and generating a reconfirmation checklist before a revised proposal.               | Recover undecided enquiries without repeating stale terms.                            |
| 16  | **Owner-approved alternative suggestions** attached to an enquiry when a requested model cannot be sourced.                   | Keep a potentially suitable buyer conversation alive.                                 |
| 17  | **A follow-up priority score** based on stated occasion, quote stage and confirmed potential contribution.                    | Help Anton spend time on the most useful next actions.                                |
| 18  | **An assortment decision view** combining genuine demand, fulfilled sales, complete costs and sample outcomes.                | Select models that can produce healthy contribution; leave incomplete inputs unknown. |
| 19  | **Verified reply templates for common objections**, linked to the relevant product FAQ/guide.                                 | Answer size, material, price and delivery questions faster and consistently.          |
| 20  | **Homepage and product performance improvements**, targeting critical images, JavaScript and navigation responsiveness.       | Reduce loading friction, particularly on slower mobile connections.                   |

Start with B01–B04 and the other correctness defects. The highest-value next feature batch is ideas 1–4, 10–11 and 19; these improve the path from interest to a useful conversation without needing a new marketing platform. Quote-link persistence or richer saved records may need a later migration; plan that separately. Email integrations, the two-ring builder, Meta/Google product attribution and publication-check tooling remain deferred.
